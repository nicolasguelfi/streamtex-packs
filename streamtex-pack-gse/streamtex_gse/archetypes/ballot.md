## Scene composition

In the **centre** of the frame, a **close-up scene**: a single
**hand-icon** (electric-blue silhouette, simple stylised flat shape — no
finger detail, no skin texture, just the silhouette of an open hand
viewed from the side) is shown placing a **small folded digital token**
into a **glowing receptacle**.

- The **hand-icon** comes from the upper-left, descending toward the
  receptacle. It is electric-blue with a subtle inner gradient.
- The **folded token** is a small abstract geometric shape (clearly a
  digital token, never a paper sheet or paper ballot — think a
  rectangular plate with a soft fold line, glowing softly amber). It is
  held between the fingertips of the hand-icon.
- The **receptacle** is a stylised box rendered in teal — a simple
  rectangular shape with rounded corners and a thin slot at the top, a
  warm amber inner glow visible through the slot.

Around the box, in the surrounding dark space, **small ambient nodes**
(teal circular nodes, low intensity, sparsely distributed) suggest the
institutional context — the vote happens within a body, not in
isolation. The nodes are connected by very thin teal lines barely
visible.

The composition is **centred** but with diagonal energy from upper-left
(hand) to lower-centre (receptacle) to upper-right (one slightly brighter
ambient node in the upper-right indicating "the institution receives the
vote").

Generous dark navy negative space.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas
- `electric-blue` — the hand-icon + faint constellation lines
- `teal` — the receptacle + the ambient nodes
- `amber` — the folded token + the warm inner glow of the receptacle
  (focal warmth)
- `white` — not used here

---

## When to use

Slides about a formal decision act: a vote, a signature, the moment a
deliberation becomes a decision. Particularly fits the slide that
articulates "the resolution submitted to your vote", or any slide where
the visual must convey "this is the moment of the act".

The composition is intimate (close-up) — use it when the act itself is
the message, not the institutional procession around it (for which see
`path-of-arrows.md`).

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- A single hand placing a single token into a single receptacle.
- Diagonal energy from hand (upper-left) to receptacle (lower-centre).
- Amber accent on the token + amber glow inside the receptacle.
- Ambient context nodes around the box, sparse and faint.

**Can be adapted**:

- The orientation of the hand (descending from upper-left default;
  could descend from upper-right if mirroring is needed).
- The shape of the token (folded plate by default; could be a small
  glowing disc, or a small geometric tag).
- The density of ambient nodes (sparse = quiet institutional moment;
  denser = a more populated chamber).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Multiple hands or multiple tokens (defeats the "single act" reading).
- A literal paper ballot with a printed crosshair / X mark (paper-era
  object, per §9 forbidden list — and the no-text rule forbids the X
  mark).
- A realistic urn or wooden ballot box (too literal; keep it as a
  geometric glowing receptacle).
- The hand showing knuckles, fingernails, or skin texture (per universal
  figure rules — abstract silhouette only).
