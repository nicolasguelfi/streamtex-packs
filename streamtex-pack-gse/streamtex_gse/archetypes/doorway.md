## Scene composition

In the **centre-right** of the frame, an **open doorway icon** rendered
as a simple rectangular frame in coral (#E07A6E), with **warm amber glow
spilling outward** into the dark space — the doorway is open, light is
coming through. The frame is geometric and clean (no decoration, no
ornate moulding).

At the **threshold of the doorway**, **two abstract figure icons**:

- **One electric-blue figure** stands just **outside** the doorway
  (slightly to the left, in the dark space), facing inward toward the
  threshold.
- **One teal figure** stands just **inside** the doorway (slightly to
  the right, in the warm glow), facing outward toward the threshold.

Both figures are simple flat silhouettes (head + shoulders, no faces, no
detail), rendered in their respective palette colours with subtle inner
gradients.

Between them, at the threshold, **a small abstract digital document icon**
(a clean geometric shape, glowing softly amber) is shown passing from one
figure to the other — the moment of recourse / accommodation /
formal exchange.

The amber glow from inside the doorway extends asymmetrically into the
left side of the frame, fading into the dark navy. Generous dark negative
space on the upper portion of the frame.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas (left side and upper portion)
- `electric-blue` — outside figure
- `teal` — inside figure
- `coral` — the doorway frame itself (the focal warmth of the political
  dimension — recourse)
- `amber` — the warm glow spilling from inside + the small document icon
  at the threshold

---

## When to use

Political-dimension slides: recourse, accommodation, an institutional
mechanism that opens a passage for an individual. Particularly fits
slides about an Independent Commission for Reasonable Adjustments, ombuds
mechanisms, appeal procedures, or any institutional gesture that says
"there is a door, and it is open".

This is **the canonical political-dimension archetype** — coral is the
load-bearing colour, not amber. Amber appears as the spillover glow but
not as the dominant element.

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- A single open doorway, asymmetrically placed (centre-right).
- Coral as the frame colour (not electric-blue, not teal).
- Two figures at the threshold (one outside, one inside).
- A small document icon being exchanged at the threshold.
- Warm glow spilling from inside the doorway.

**Can be adapted**:

- The placement of the figures (both can be outside if the message is
  "the door is open and waiting"; both can be inside if the recourse
  has been received).
- The exchanged icon (digital document by default; could be a small
  glowing key for "access granted", or omitted entirely for "an open
  door, no transaction yet").
- The width of the doorway (narrow = exceptional recourse; wide =
  routine mechanism).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Closed door (defeats the recourse message; if you want to show
  closure, use a different visual entirely — but generally GSE avoids
  the closed-door reading).
- Aggressive coral / red tones (the political dimension is recourse,
  warmth without aggression — never alarm-red, never blood-red).
- A figure with detailed face or expression (per universal figure rules).
- Realistic door with hinges, handle, doorknob, woodgrain (too literal
  — keep it as a geometric icon).
