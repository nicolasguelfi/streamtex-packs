## Scene composition

A **mature variant of the bridge archetype** (see `bridge.md`).

Two stylised academic building icons stand on opposite cliff edges in
the middle of the frame, separated by a deep dark abyss in the
lower-centre — **the same composition as the title slide**, but evolved:

- **Both buildings are fully lit** — the classical (electric-blue,
  pediment + columns) and the modern (teal, rectangle + rounded
  corners) buildings now show many more lit windows than in the title
  variant. They glow softly from within, suggesting active life.
- **The amber bridge is persistent and brighter** — the luminous arc
  spans the abyss with stronger glow, the amber halo extending further
  into the dark space than in the title.
- **The constellation above has matured** — more nodes (8-12 instead of
  3-4), more thin teal lines weaving between them, multiple amber accent
  nodes (2-3 instead of 1) distributed across the constellation,
  suggesting a fully developed digital network.

**A subtle outward glow** extends from the buildings and the bridge into
the surrounding dark canvas — a faint warm-cool blend that softens the
edges of the composition without competing with the focal elements.

Generous dark navy negative space remains in the abyss and the
peripheral sky, but the overall luminosity of the scene is **higher
than in the bridge / title variant** — the institution is now alive and
networked.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas (still 50-60 %, slightly less than in
  the title because of the increased luminosity)
- `electric-blue` — left (classical) building + half the constellation
  nodes + outward glow
- `teal` — right (modern) building + most constellation lines and
  nodes + outward glow
- `amber` — the bridge arc (brighter than in the title) + 2-3 accent
  nodes in the constellation
- `white` — sparingly, as faint pin-point highlights on the brightest
  amber elements (the bridge crest, the brightest constellation node)

---

## When to use

The final slide of a deck — the closing image after the resolution /
vote / decision has been articulated. The message is "this is what the
institution looks like once the proposed direction has been adopted and
matured". The visual continuity with the title (same buildings, same
abyss, same bridge) creates a narrative arc: opening → journey →
closing.

This archetype only makes sense **when the title slide used `bridge.md`**
— it is the visual closure of that opening. If the title used a
different archetype, choose a different closing image.

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical to the bridge**:

- Same two building icons in the same positions (left = classical,
  right = modern).
- Same abyss in the lower-centre.
- Same bridge spanning the abyss (amber glowing arc).
- Same diagonal/horizontal balance (the bridge arc is the centring
  element).

**Can be adapted (these are the "mature" deltas)**:

- Number of lit windows in each building (more than the title — at
  least 1.5× as many).
- Brightness of the bridge (brighter than the title; halo extends further).
- Density of the constellation above (more nodes, more lines, more
  amber accents than the title).
- Slight overall luminosity increase (the dark canvas remains, but
  shapes glow more).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Different buildings than the title (defeats the visual continuity).
- Tipped or broken bridge (the closing must affirm, not negate).
- Pure white or daylight sky (per §9 forbidden list).
- Too much amber dominance (still the focal warmth, never the canvas).
- Adding human figures on the bridge (creates a new focal point that
  competes with the institutional message).
