## Scene composition

In the **centre** of the frame, **four colored icon discs** arranged at
the four cardinal positions (north, east, south, west) of an invisible
circle, forming a **closed cycle**:

- North disc: teal (#2EC4B6) with subtle inner gradient.
- East disc: electric-blue (#7AB8F5).
- South disc: amber (#F39C12) — the focal warmth of the cycle.
- West disc: coral (#E07A6E).

Each disc holds **a single small symbolic glyph** rendered as a clean flat
icon (no readable text, no number) — the glyph is just enough to
distinguish the phase but never literal: a small geometric shape (a
triangle, a wave, a small rectangle, a chevron). Glyphs are in white with
soft inner gradient against their disc.

Between each pair of discs, **a single thin curved arrow** in a soft
teal-white tone follows the circular path, indicating direction of
progression (clockwise by default).

The cycle is **centred and balanced** — no element dominates. Generous
dark navy negative space surrounds the cycle, with a faint teal halo
suggesting the cycle's continuous nature.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas surrounding the cycle
- `electric-blue` — one disc + gradient halos
- `teal` — one disc + the connecting arrows
- `amber` — one disc (the focal warmth, traditionally the "act" or
  "decide" phase)
- `coral` — one disc (the "review" or "feedback" phase)
- `white` — the four glyphs at low intensity

---

## When to use

Slides about iterative processes: continuous improvement, governance
cycles, audit / review cycles, deployment loops, curriculum-revision
cycles. The composition reads in 2 seconds as "a process that keeps going
— no start, no end".

This archetype is **balanced and symmetric** — use it when the message *is*
balance among phases (no phase more important than another).

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- Four discs at cardinal positions forming a closed cycle.
- One arrow between each pair of discs, all curving in the same direction.
- Each disc carries one minimal glyph — no readable text.
- Symmetric, centred composition.

**Can be adapted**:

- The colours of the four discs can be **reassigned** to match the
  semantics of the phases (e.g., decisional = amber if the focal phase is
  the decision; technical = teal if the focal phase is execution).
- The glyphs in each disc — choose simple geometric shapes that hint at
  the phase without naming it.
- The direction of rotation (clockwise default; counter-clockwise allowed
  if the message requires it).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Three discs (breaks the four-phase reading; use a triangle composition
  instead).
- More than four discs (becomes crowded and loses the "cycle" reading).
- Open path rather than closed loop (defeats the semantic; use
  `path-of-arrows.md` instead for a procession).
- Numbered glyphs or readable labels (no text rule).
