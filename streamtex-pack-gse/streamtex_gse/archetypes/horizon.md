## Scene composition

In the **lower-left third** of the frame, three small abstract figure
icons stand close together on a soft elevated plateau, shown from behind
(no faces, no detail — simple flat silhouettes of head + shoulders,
electric-blue and teal alternating, soft inner gradient). They look out
and upward toward the upper-right.

From their plateau, **an ascending bridge / luminous walkway** extends
toward the upper-right horizon, ascending diagonally. The bridge is woven
from **three glowing parallel ribbons** twisted softly together: warm
amber at the top, teal in the middle, electric-blue at the bottom. The
three ribbons flow together along the bridge length, with subtle gradient
glow extending into the dark space around them. The bridge opens outward
as it nears the horizon — never closing.

At the upper-right end of the bridge, **a constellation of small teal
circular nodes** connected by thin teal lines, with one bright amber
accent node at its centre — a hopeful horizon of digital intelligence.

Soft atmospheric glow under the figures' plateau and along the bridge.
Generous dark navy negative space.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas
- `electric-blue` — figures (some) + lower ribbon of the bridge
- `teal` — figures (others) + middle ribbon + constellation lines and nodes
- `amber` — top ribbon of the bridge + central focal accent in the
  constellation (the horizon point)
- `white` — sparingly, as a faint pin-point highlight at the horizon

---

## When to use

Problem-statement slides, opening slides for a multi-commitment
proposition, slides where the message is "we (community) commit together
to a long-term horizon, and the path forward is woven from N distinct
commitments".

The number of ribbons can match the number of commitments (typically 3,
sometimes 2 or 4 — never more than 4, as the bridge would lose readability).

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- Figures in the lower-left third, looking toward the upper-right.
- Diagonal upward composition (lower-left → upper-right ascending energy).
- Bridge woven from multiple parallel ribbons.
- Horizon at the upper-right end with a constellation focal element.
- Generous dark negative space.

**Can be adapted**:

- Number of figures (1 = individual decision; 3–5 = small community;
  6–8 = larger cohort).
- Number of ribbons in the bridge (2–4) — typically matches the number of
  commitments being illustrated.
- Density of the constellation at the horizon (sparse = early stage;
  denser = mature destination).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Reversed diagonal (upper-left → lower-right reads as decline, not horizon).
- Closed bridge / archway at the horizon (must stay open-ended).
- Figures with faces or expressions.
- Bright sky behind the horizon (must stay dark navy).
