## Scene composition

In the **lower-left** of the frame, a single abstract figure icon shown
from behind (no face, no detail — simple flat silhouette of head +
shoulders, electric-blue with subtle gradient), standing in front of a
floating translucent rectangular panel.

The **panel** is rendered as a soft teal glass-like rectangle with a
subtle inner glow, floating mid-frame. On the panel, **a single ascending
curve** (electric-blue gradient) rises from lower-left to upper-right,
with three small bar-shape icons below the curve. The figure has one arm
slightly raised, gesturing at the curve.

From the upper-right end of the curve, **small circular nodes**
(electric-blue and teal, with one amber accent) lift upward and connect by
thin teal lines, forming a small constellation that resolves at the top of
the frame into **the stylised silhouette of academic spires and rooftops**
— clean geometric shapes (a small dome, a tower, a low pediment), in muted
electric-blue, glowing softly against the dark sky.

Atmospheric soft glow around the panel and around the rising
constellation. Generous dark navy negative space.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas
- `electric-blue` — figure + ascending curve + spires silhouette
- `teal` — panel fill + constellation lines and most nodes
- `amber` — single accent node in the constellation (the AI presence)
- `white` — sparingly, faint highlight at the top of the spires

---

## When to use

Slides where the message is "the new digital reality is here — data leads
to a transformed institutional environment". The composition narrates a
small story (read data → nodes lift → resolve into built environment) but
without literal narrative figures or text.

Particularly fits "historic moment" slides, "context" slides, and slides
where data evidence translates into institutional consequence.

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- Single figure in the lower-left, observing.
- Floating data panel mid-frame.
- Vertical lift from the panel's data into a constellation that resolves
  into the institutional silhouette at the top.
- Three vertical zones (figure low-left → panel mid → spires top).

**Can be adapted**:

- The specific data shape on the panel (curve + bars is the default;
  could be a single curve, or three trend lines, etc.).
- The institutional silhouette at the top (academic spires for
  university; could be a generic skyline for non-academic uses).
- The figure's posture (arm raised gesturing; or hands at sides observing).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Multiple figures (breaks the single-observer reading).
- Multiple panels (breaks single focal element).
- Bright/coloured sky behind the spires.
- Realistic charts with readable numbers or labels (no text rule).
