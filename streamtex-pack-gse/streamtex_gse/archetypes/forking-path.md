## Scene composition

In the **centre-left** of the frame, a single abstract figure shown from
behind (no face, no detail — simple flat silhouette of head + shoulders,
electric-blue with subtle gradient), standing at a fork in the path.

- **The left path** is muted dark blue — almost merging with the
  background — a path that fades into the dark. This is the past / status
  quo / dead-end.
- **The right path** is bright teal with an amber glow at its far end —
  this is the future / alternative / chosen direction.

The figure is **gently turned toward the right path** — body language
already oriented toward the choice.

In the **upper-right quadrant**, a constellation of teal and electric-blue
nodes connected by thin teal lines, with two amber accent nodes — the
knowledge / digital network that awaits at the end of the chosen path.

In the **upper-left quadrant**, subtle horizontal lines in muted dark blue
— a faint history texture, low contrast, not competing for attention.

Asymmetric composition. Generous dark navy negative space.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas
- `electric-blue` — the figure + secondary nodes in the upper-right
  constellation + faint history texture (upper-left, very low opacity)
- `teal` — the right (chosen) path + most constellation nodes + lines
- `amber` — the glow at the far end of the right path + two accent nodes
  in the constellation
- `coral` — not used (this is not a political-dimension scene)
- `white` — not used

---

## When to use

Slides where the message is "we face a choice between continuing on the
present path (which is fading) or taking an alternative (which is brighter
and connects to the wider network)". Particularly fits status-quo slides,
decision-justification slides, and slides arguing that inaction has a
cost.

This archetype is **explicitly asymmetric** to mirror the asymmetry of the
choice — the right path is preferable, and the composition shows it.

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- Single figure at a fork.
- Two paths: one muted/fading, one bright/glowing.
- Amber glow at the end of the bright path.
- Constellation in the upper-right (the network that awaits).
- Faint history texture in the upper-left.
- Figure's posture turned toward the bright path.

**Can be adapted**:

- The angle of divergence between the two paths (sharper angle = sharper
  choice; gentler = more nuanced choice).
- The intensity of the amber glow at the far end (subtle for "early
  signal"; brighter for "clear destination").
- The density of the constellation (sparse for "potential"; denser for
  "established").

**Forbidden variations**:

- Three or more paths (breaks the binary choice reading).
- Both paths equally bright (defeats the asymmetry that carries the
  message).
- Figure facing the camera or facing the dark path (breaks the implicit
  recommendation).
- Adding a barrier or wall on the dark path (too literal — the fade alone
  carries the meaning).
