## Scene composition

In the **centre** of the frame, a single stylised academic icon — a soft
electric-blue classical pediment with two columns, slightly glowing, clean
flat geometric shape with subtle gradient depth. The icon rests on a
subtle teal circular platform / base.

Around the central icon, **a circle of six small abstract figure icons**
(no faces, no detail — just simple flat silhouettes of head + shoulders,
alternating electric-blue and teal), arranged at equal angles around the
central icon. Each figure is connected to the central icon by a thin
glowing teal line / thread. The figures face inward toward the icon (their
backs to the viewer).

Above the central icon, **a luminous amber circular node** — a small
bright "spirit" light, glowing softly, with a faint amber halo extending
outward — symbol of the central knowledge mission held in common.

In the upper part of the frame, a small constellation of teal nodes
connected by thin lines arcs across the dark sky — suggesting the wider
digital network this institution participates in.

**Symmetric composition** centred on the icon. Generous dark navy negative
space.

## Palette usage

- `bg-navy` — dominant canvas
- `electric-blue` — central institution icon + half the figures
- `teal` — central platform + half the figures + connecting threads +
  upper constellation
- `amber` — the spirit-light above the icon (single focal warmth)
- `white` — not used here

---

## When to use

Slides where the message is "we form a community / federation around a
shared mission". Particularly fits the "knowledge first" beat,
foundational-principle slides, alliance / partnership slides, governance
slides emphasising elected academic leadership of a body.

This is the only archetype in the gallery that uses **symmetric
composition** (most others prefer asymmetric / diagonal). Use it
intentionally when the message *is* symmetry / equality / common-purpose.

---

## Variation rules

**Must stay structurally identical**:

- Central institutional icon.
- Circle of figures at equal angles around it, facing inward.
- Each figure connected to the centre by a thin thread.
- Single amber spirit-light above the centre.
- Symmetric composition.

**Can be adapted**:

- Number of figures (5–7 — fewer feels sparse; more feels crowded).
- Specific institution icon at the centre (pediment + columns is the
  default for "academic"; could be a simple geometric block for "agency",
  a network-of-rooms icon for "consortium", etc.).
- Density of the upper constellation (sparse = local; denser = networked
  worldwide).

**Forbidden variations**:

- Asymmetric placement of the central icon (breaks the federation reading).
- Figures facing outward (reads as dispersion, not federation).
- Multiple amber accents (breaks single-focal-element rule).
- Adding text labels naming members (no text rule).
